This &nbsp; policy &nbsp; governs &nbsp; on &nbsp; all &nbsp; the &nbsp; public &nbsp; facing &nbsp; JNU &nbsp; website(s) &nbsp; created &nbsp; using &nbsp; the approved &nbsp; Content &nbsp; Management &nbsp; All website(s) hosted on JNU Servers or any arranged ICT Infrastructure must adhere to all sections of this policy. &nbsp; JNU web contents, including its website, must be created in accordance with Government laws, &nbsp; policies, &nbsp; guidelines &nbsp; and &nbsp; JNU &nbsp; standard &nbsp; operating &nbsp; procedures; &nbsp; and &nbsp; must &nbsp; be &nbsp; sustained &nbsp; in &nbsp; a manner that it maintains quality control, manages security risks, optimizes usability, easy access and provides data consistency. This policy defines the terms and conditions for all websites utilizing JNU &nbsp; resources. &nbsp; PURPOSE The purpose of this policy is to establish JNU procedures and guidelines for operating and maintaining some high quality web standards, ease to access, secure, compliant with Government of India website guidelines and service-oriented World Wide Web presence of the University. CIS internal central ICT facility of the University will support and assure development of the University’s webpages along with the coordinated efforts of various Schools/ Centre/ Departments of the University. This Web Policy describes the use of website(s) of the University &nbsp; in &nbsp; accordance &nbsp; with &nbsp; various &nbsp; IT &nbsp; standards &nbsp; and &nbsp; policies &nbsp; of &nbsp; Govt. &nbsp; of &nbsp; India &nbsp; and &nbsp; guidelines of the &nbsp; University. CIS &nbsp; of &nbsp; JNU &nbsp; is &nbsp; responsible &nbsp; for &nbsp; managing &nbsp; and &nbsp; maintaining &nbsp; the &nbsp; World &nbsp; Wide &nbsp; Web &nbsp; presence &nbsp; of &nbsp; the University, in concurrences of the website committee composed from various departments of JNU and also as advised by CIS Advisory committee of the &nbsp; University. A Website Committee should also be constituted with the approval of the competent authority in order to manage and address the new issues of websites. It will advise the Competent Authority for changes to the guidelines, if any and help incorporating new operating procedures and functional requirements, and for submitting the technical status reports &nbsp; for &nbsp; the &nbsp; consideration &nbsp; of &nbsp; JNU &nbsp; Competent &nbsp; Authority &nbsp; prior &nbsp; to &nbsp; the &nbsp; implementation &nbsp; of &nbsp; new platform, &nbsp; policies. &nbsp; SCOPE OF &nbsp; POLICY This policy applies to all including: Vice Chancellor Rectors, Registrar, Finance Officer, CoE, &nbsp; DoA Directors, Deans &amp; Chairpersons, &nbsp; Co-coordinators Faculty &nbsp; Members Staff Students and research &nbsp; scholars Contractual Staff or other associated manpower deputed in the &nbsp; University ICT Infrastructure: All web servers connected to, and web pages hosted on, the .ac.in, edu.in, res.in domains of &nbsp; JNU. We do not sell or share any personally identifiable information volunteered on the JNU-Website to any third party (public/private). Any information provided to this website will be protected from loss, misuse, unauthorized access or disclosure, alteration, or destruction. We gather certain information about the User, such as Internet protocol (IP) addresses, domain name, browser type, operating system, the date and time of the visit and the pages visited. We make no attempt to link these addresses with the identity of individuals visiting our site unless an attempt to damage the site has been detected. &nbsp; Hyper-linking Policy 1.
