Cookies Visitors should be aware that non-personal information and data may be automatically collected by Akshaya Patra&#39;s Website through the use of cookies. Cookies are not programs that come onto a visitor&#39;s system and damage files. Generally, cookies work by assigning a unique number to the visitor that has no meaning outside the assigning site. Akshaya Patra uses cookie technology only to obtain non-personal information from its online visitors in order to improve visitors&#39; online experience and facilitate their visit within our site.
