# ADHIKAAR Gold-Set Adjudication Rubric (47 DPDP duties)

How to use: for each site, open its policy text file (policy_*.txt), read it, and in
gold_labels_blank.csv assign ONE verdict per duty in the 'verdict' column. Do NOT consult
the Adhikaar output; judge only from the policy text and the statute. Optionally note the
supporting sentence in 'evidence_note'.

Verdict options:
- Compliant: the policy clearly states or commits to the required practice.
- Partial: the topic is mentioned but is vague, incomplete, or conditional.
- Gap: the practice is required and expected, but is absent from the policy.
- NotApplicable: the duty does not apply to this type of site.
- NotVerifiable: an internal control that cannot be judged from public text alone.

Note: if a site has no policy at all, most disclosure duties are 'Gap'.
