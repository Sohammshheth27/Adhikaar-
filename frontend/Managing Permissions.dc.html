A site asked for something it doesn't need? Here's how to say no.

Two minutes, with pictures. These steps work in Chrome and other Chromium browsers such as Edge and Brave.

Keeping your browsing private

1

Don't tap Allow reflexively. Take a second to ask why the site needs it.

2

Deny anything the site's purpose doesn't explain. A news site has no clear need for your microphone.

3

Review your site permissions occasionally: Settings, then Privacy and security, then Site settings.

4

Prefer "Ask" over "Allow" as your default, so every request needs your decision.

5

Let Adhikar flag requests that look unusual for the kind of site you're on.

Saying no is your right. So is asking them to delete what they took.

Adhikar drafts that request for you, under the DPDP Act, right on your device.
